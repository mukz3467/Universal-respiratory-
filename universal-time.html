Christmas Countdown and World Clock FAQs

When is Christmas 2026?

Christmas Day is December 25, 2026. The countdown shows the remaining time and automatically targets the next Christmas once the date has passed.

How does the Christmas countdown work?

The countdown calculates the time remaining until December 25 and updates every second in your browser.

What does UTC mean?

UTC stands for Coordinated Universal Time. It is the global time standard used to coordinate time across regions and systems.

How is UTC different from local time?

Local time depends on a location's time zone and may change with daylight-saving rules. UTC provides a common reference for comparing times between locations.

Is this a free online world clock?

Yes. You can check UTC and compare the time in the listed cities for free, without installing an app.

Does the world clock account for daylight saving time?

The city clocks use the browser’s built-in time-zone data, which generally applies daylight-saving rules where they are observed.
