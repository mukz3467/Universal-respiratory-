About the Christmas countdown

This free Christmas countdown timer calculates the time remaining until December 25, 2026. It updates every second using your device clock and adjusts to your local time zone. No account or app installation is needed.

When is Christmas 2026?

Christmas Day falls on Friday, December 25, 2026. Use the countdown to plan celebrations, decorations, gifts and family gatherings.

How does the timer work?

The timer compares the current date and time on your device with the start of Christmas Day in your local time zone. If the date has passed, it automatically counts toward the next Christmas.
