Free Online Word Counter for Essays, Articles, and More

Use this word counter with character count to check the length of an essay, assignment, article, email, social media post, or other text. Paste or type your text into the box above and the counts update as you edit. You do not need to create an account or install software.

Check Word Count and Character Count Instantly

The tool displays your total word count and character count, including a separate count for characters without spaces. It also measures sentences, lines, paragraphs, and estimated reading and speaking time. These details can help students check an essay's word limit, writers prepare content, and professionals fit text into length restrictions.

Character Counter Without Spaces (With Both Counts)

Need a character counter without spaces? This tool shows the character total both with spaces and after whitespace is removed, so you can use the right figure for your assignment, publishing brief, or form. It also works as a character counter with spaces, and the results update while you type or paste text.

Word Count Checker for Essays and Assignments

Before submitting an essay or assignment, paste your draft into the counter and check the total words against your required minimum or maximum. This tool reports the count; you should still follow your school's or instructor's rules about what is included in the official word limit, such as headings, citations, and references.

Word Counter for Articles, Emails, and Social Media

Use this online word count checker to review blog posts, emails, captions, reports, and other writing before publishing or sending. The live word count and character count help you check length requirements without manually counting each word. For changing capitalization in your text, try our free text case converter. Visit the OmniTool Global homepage to explore more free browser-based tools.

How Word Count Is Calculated

The counter uses browser-based text segmentation to estimate words, then reports characters with spaces and without spaces, sentences, lines, paragraphs, and estimated reading and speaking time. Different writing systems and platform rules can count text differently, so use the rules provided by your school, publisher, or platform when an exact official count is required.

Does This Tool Check Grammar?

This page is a word and character counter, not a grammar checker or vocabulary corrector. It measures text length and structure so you can review your writing; it does not claim to detect grammar mistakes or suggest vocabulary changes.

Private, Browser-Based Text Counting

Your text is processed in your browser by this page's counting script rather than being submitted to a word-counting service. Avoid entering highly sensitive information into any online tool unless you have reviewed its privacy and security details.

Word Counter and Character Counter FAQs

Does the character counter count spaces?

The character total includes spaces and punctuation as characters. Use the displayed character metric to check the length of your text for forms, posts, and writing limits.

Can I count words in multilingual text?

The tool is designed for English and multilingual text, including Roman Urdu and Hindi. Results can vary for languages that do not separate words with spaces.

Is my text uploaded to a server?

Text analysis runs in your browser, so your text is not intentionally sent to a server by the counter. Avoid entering sensitive information into any website unless you have reviewed its privacy practices.

How do I count both words and characters online?

Paste or type your text into the free online word counter. The tool updates the available text metrics as you edit.
