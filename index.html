Which tool should I use to count words and characters?

Use the online word and character counter to check words, characters with or without spaces, sentences, paragraphs and lines.

How do I change uppercase text to lowercase?

Open the text case converter to switch between uppercase, lowercase, title case, sentence case and common coding formats.

Yes. The universal time and world clock tool helps you check UTC and compare global time zones.

Is there a free online stopwatch?

Yes. Use the online stopwatch and countdown timer to measure elapsed time and track laps or countdowns.
