## Next practical task: use first-party Search Console data

Open [Google Search Console](https://search.google.com/search-console) and select the verified OmniTool property:

1. Go to **Performance → Search results**.
2. Review the last 28 days and last 3 months separately.
3. Export the **Queries** and **Pages** tables as CSV.
4. Prioritize queries with impressions but low CTR, and queries already appearing for the relevant tool page.
5. Compare country and device before deciding whether to target the United States, Bangladesh, Pakistan or a broader audience.

Google documents that Search Console reports show the queries, clicks, impressions and position associated with your site: https://search.google.com/search-console/about.

Do not add keyword repetitions just to increase frequency. Keep one main search intent per page, answer that intent clearly, and use the query export to decide the next on-page edit. No ranking or traffic outcome is guaranteed.
